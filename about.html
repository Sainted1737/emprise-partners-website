About Emprise Partners

A prime contractor for facility support and site services, serving clients nationwide.

One accountable prime contractor

Emprise Partners LLC is a prime contractor delivering facility support and site services to federal, state, local and commercial clients nationwide.

We hold the contract and oversee every job, supported by a vetted network of trade partners. Instead of managing multiple vendors, relationships and agreements, our clients work with one accountable prime contractor.

Our mission is to deliver reliable service, create strong partnerships, and give our clients confidence that their facilities are cared for, no matter the service or location.

How we deliver

Emprise Partners sets the standards, coordinates the work and inspects the results, with one line of communication for every property.

  1. One point of accountability

    One relationship across every service you need, with centralized contract administration and invoicing.

  2. Oversight and supervision

    We assign each scope to a qualified partner, set the schedule and standards, and supervise the work through completion.

  3. Compliance

    Insurance, licensing and documentation tracked and verified for every partner before they set foot on site.

  4. Quality assurance

    Inspections and reporting hold every job to the same standard, at every location.

  5. Responsiveness

    Prompt replies, from bid questions to field issues.
