Frequently asked questions

What does Emprise Partners do?

Emprise Partners is a facility support and site services company. We hold the contract and oversee every job, supported by a vetted network of trade partners.

How is your model different from hiring individual vendors?

Instead of managing multiple contracts, invoices and relationships, you work with one accountable prime contractor. Emprise Partners handles contract management, compliance, communication, quality assurance and performance management.

Who do you work with?

Federal, state and local government agencies, along with commercial clients such as property management companies and multi-location operators.

Where are you located and what areas do you serve?

We're headquartered in Maitland, Florida, and serve federal, state, local and commercial clients across the United States.

How do you ensure quality and compliance?

Emprise Partners sets the standards, supervises the work and inspects the results. Every trade partner is vetted for qualifications, licensing and insurance, and we maintain the documentation so you have one point of oversight.

How do I request a proposal?

Use the form on this page, email info@emprise-partners.com, or call 800.254.0658. We'll review your needs and follow up to scope the right service program.
